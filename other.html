Framework Laptop Repair


I recently tried to fix a Framework laptop which had a broken mainboard. After taking it to a laptop repair shop, they said they couldn't fix it because they didn't have the tools needed to reflash the embedded controller (EC) firmware. Determined to bring the laptop back to life, I decided to look online and found Framework's documentation about reflashing the EC from a USB-C port using an FTDI chip and a USB-C breakout board, along with this blog post which documented this process. After repeated failed attempts, I concluded that the EC was broken to the point that it was no longer properly controlling the USB ports, and thus UART communication was no longer working over said port. In the future, I plan to solder wires to the dedicated debug port on the PCB, which has a more direct connection to the EC.
